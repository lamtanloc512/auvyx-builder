Book now
Do I pay when I book?

No. Booking is free and you pay after your appointment. Memberships and packages are paid at checkout.

How early should I arrive?

Five minutes early is perfect. Arriving more than ten minutes late may shorten your treatment.

Can I change or cancel?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before. Use the link in your confirmation text.

Do you patch test colour?

Yes. We recommend a patch test 48 hours before any colour service, and we will book it for you.

Can I choose my stylist?

Yes. Pick one on the booking form, or book with them directly from their profile.
